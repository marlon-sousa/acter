Far-end field element probe

Every field below behaves exactly as Acter's far-end line does: every key is prevented, nothing is inserted locally, and the text and caret are written by script to stand for what a far end drew. They differ only in role and ARIA. Type ech then press Tab.

B. textbox plus aria-autocomplete inline
C. combobox plus aria-autocomplete inline
D. searchbox
E. textbox, completion selects what it added

G. textbox plus a live region fed on EVERY answer, not only the completion
H. textbox, completion selects what it added and collapses the caret straight after
I. textbox unchanged, plus aria-controls pointing at a listbox whose selection moves
J. the full ARIA combobox: expanded, aria-controls, aria-activedescendant
K. as I, but one option, replaced each time, with the position info suppressed
